# Contribution Guide

Puppetblox is very welcoming to outside help! But before you do so, please read the guidelines we've set out to make
sure contributions are high quality and that we all have a good time working on the project.

## Code/Documentation Contributions

### AI Generation Policy

We understand that AI and LLMs in programming are now commonplace, however, Puppetblox will not accept pull requests
that are written with or co-authored by an AI. These sorts of contributions signify that effort was not put in to the
pull request, and do not inspire any confidence that what is being contributed is functional. Intentionally concealing
AI usage will result in a ban from contributions in the future upon discovery.
